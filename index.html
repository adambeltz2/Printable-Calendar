Your events

Type dates and text directly into the rows below, like a spreadsheet — or copy cells from Excel/Google Sheets and paste into the first Date box to fill in several rows at once. Dates can be 6/12/2026 or 6/12 (no year = repeats yearly, like a birthday), or type a weekday name like Tuesday for something that happens every week. "Who" is optional — type a name and it'll be matched or added to Family automatically.
